Copy the folder, double-click, teach

Each app is one folder. Put it on a flash drive, a network drive or your Documents. It runs in Edge or Chrome with no admin rights and no internet.

Nothing leaves your computer

No accounts, no sign-ins, no analytics. Settings stay in your own browser on your own computer.

Open for anyone to read

Every app is plain HTML and JavaScript, nothing hidden or compiled. Open the folder and read it, or ask your IT team to. We'd encourage it.

Is my copy the real one?

Only copies that match the fingerprints published here are ours. If someone passed you a copy, check it here in a few seconds. Nothing is uploaded.
