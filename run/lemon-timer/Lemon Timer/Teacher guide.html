Lemon Timer: teacher guide

Version 0.4.1. A classroom timer with a cartoon teacher at her desk. Every lemon is one minute: she takes a lemon at the start of each minute and eats it, bite by sour bite. When the time is up she goes to the chalkboard and writes your message in NSW Foundation cursive.

What's in the folder

Lemon Timer\
  Start Lemon Timer.cmd   double-click to start
  Start in browser.html   use this if the .cmd file is blocked
  Teacher guide.html      this guide
  System\                 the app itself (no need to open)

Copy the whole Lemon Timer folder to a flash drive, a network drive or your Documents. It runs on any Windows computer with Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome. Nothing is installed, no admin rights are needed, and it works without internet.

Running a timer

  1. If you received a .zip file, right-click it and choose Extract All first. It will not work from inside the zip.
  2. Double-click Start Lemon Timer. (If Windows says the file is blocked, use Start in browser.html.)
  3. Type the Message she will write on the board. Press Enter for a second line. One line is written low on the board; for two or three lines she fetches her step stool.
  4. Set the Hours and Minutes, then click Start (or press Space).
  5. Click Full screen for the class. The buttons fade after a few seconds; move the mouse to bring them back.
  • Pause / Resume stops and restarts the clock. Reset puts all the lemons back.
  • +1 min gives her one more lemon, even while the timer is running.
  • Up to 30 lemons sit on the desk. Longer timers add crates of ten beside the desk; she fetches one when the desk runs low.
  • Countdown shows or hides the small clock. Sound turns the sounds on or off.
  • Save keeps the message and time under Saved timers for next lesson.

Choosing the teacher

Click Teacher to change her style, hair, outfit, colours, roundness, head size, eye size, mouth and extras. Click Use this teacher to keep the change. Back to her original choice restores the original design.

Settings, saved timers and the teacher are remembered by the browser on each computer.
