Terms and privacy

Short and plain, because the apps are.

Using the apps

The apps are free to use in classrooms and for education. Please keep the credit: the About box and CREDITS.txt in each app say who made it. We are not a company and we don't sell anything.

No warranty

The apps are provided as they are, without any warranty. We have tried hard to make them reliable, but we can't promise they will suit every purpose or never go wrong, and we aren't responsible for any loss that comes from using them.

Official copies

Only copies that match the fingerprints (SHA-256 checksums) published on lennonapps.com are ours. A copy that doesn't match has been changed by someone else, and we can't vouch for it. You can check a copy here.

Open code

Each app is plain HTML and JavaScript that anyone can open and read. We encourage schools and IT teams to look before they use them.

Privacy: the apps

The apps run entirely on your computer. They have no accounts, no sign-ins, no analytics and no tracking, and they don't send anything over the internet. Settings you save stay in your browser on that computer.

Privacy: this website

This site has no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no forms. It loads nothing from other websites. Check a copy works out fingerprints inside your browser; your files are never uploaded.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep ordinary server logs, such as IP addresses, for security; see GitHub's privacy statement. We don't receive or look at them.
