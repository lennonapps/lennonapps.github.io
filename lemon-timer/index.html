Lemon Timer

A classroom timer where every minute is a lemon.

Download for Windows (0.4.1, 0.25 MB zip) Try it in your browser

Version 0.4.1 · Teacher guide

A cartoon teacher sits at her desk with a row of lemons. Every lemon is one minute: at the start of each minute she takes a lemon and eats it, bite by sour bite.

When the time is up she walks to the chalkboard and writes your message in NSW Foundation cursive, fetching her step stool for a longer one.

  • Timers from a minute to hours: longer ones bring out crates of lemons
  • Pause, reset, and +1 minute even while it runs
  • Your message on the board, on one to three lines
  • Full screen for the class; the buttons fade away
  • Saved timers for the ones you use every day
  • Sound on or off

Getting started

  1. Download the zip, then right-click it and choose Extract All. It won't run from inside the zip.
  2. Copy the Lemon Timer folder wherever you like: a flash drive, a network drive or your Documents.
  3. Double-click Start Lemon Timer.cmd. If Windows says the file is blocked, open Start in browser.html instead: it's the same app in a browser tab.

Made for Windows with Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome. Nothing is installed, no admin rights are needed, and it works without internet. Settings are kept in your browser on that computer only.
